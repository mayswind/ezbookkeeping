2. What we collect

  • Your account: user name, email address, display name, language, currency and similar preferences, an optional picture, and two-factor settings if you turn them on. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
  • Records you enter: accounts, transactions, categories and tags, notes and attached pictures, items and stock, locations, customers (the names, phone numbers, email addresses and notes you type), sales, repayments and receipt details, and the team members you invite with their roles.
  • Activity records: a log of changes made by managers and staff in a business (who, what, when, and from which network address), so owners can see who did what. Transactions recorded by someone other than the owner carry that person's name in their comment.
  • Technical data: network address, time, the page or action requested, time zone and browser details, kept in server logs and used to run, secure and troubleshoot the service and to limit repeated failed logins.
  • Email: we use your address to send messages you ask for, such as a password reset. We do not send marketing email.
  • Browser storage: your login and preferences are kept in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers in the app. If you use map features, your browser asks the map provider for map tiles, which shows it your network address.

3. Why we use it

  • to provide the service you signed up for (running your account, storing and showing your records, producing reports and receipts);
  • to keep the service safe: preventing abuse, limiting repeated failed logins, finding and fixing faults;
  • to follow the law, and to protect our rights if there is a dispute;
  • for anything else only with your consent, which you can withdraw.
